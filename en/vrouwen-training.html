FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need any experience?

No. All women's classes are accessible to complete beginners. You'll learn everything from the ground up.

Are the coaches women?

It varies by class. Ask us via WhatsApp — we'll tell you exactly who will be guiding you and what the group looks like.

Will I train with men?

In the women-specific training: no, it's its own group. In other classes like kickboxing, men and women sometimes train together in a respectful environment.

What's the atmosphere like?

Open, positive and judgement-free. That's what our female members value most.

What do I need for my first class?

Sports clothing and a water bottle. Gloves are available to borrow for free on your first visit.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need experience?

No. All our classes are suitable for beginners. You learn everything step by step.

Is MMA dangerous for beginners?

MMA is safe when you learn the technique gradually. In beginner classes there is no free sparring.

What should I bring?

Sports clothing and water are enough. You can borrow gloves for your first session.

Can I train multiple sports?

Yes. With one membership you have access to multiple sports and training days.

Is there a registration fee?

No. There is no registration fee.

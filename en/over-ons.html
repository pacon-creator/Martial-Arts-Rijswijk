About us

Built for people who never thought martial arts was for them.

What started as a passion project by a handful of dedicated athletes in Rijswijk has grown into the most accessible martial arts gym in the region. Not because we're the biggest — but because we never forgot why we started.

Mission

Our mission and values

Vechtsportschool Rijswijk was founded with one goal: to create a place where people of every level — beginner, parent, professional, student — feel welcome and genuinely improve. Not just as athletes. As people.

Respect

On the mat and beyond. New members are welcomed. Experienced members remember what it was like.

Progress

Every training session is a step forward. Fast or slow — it doesn't matter. What counts is that you move.
